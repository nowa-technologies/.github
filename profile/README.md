# Nowa Team

Building the calm-tech companion for a new generation of childhood.

---

**NOWA** is an AI-powered virtual pet device for children 3-10 — a Gameboy-inspired handheld where caring for a digital companion quietly builds real-world habits, emotional regulation, and executive function. The pet is emotionally real to the child, structurally reassuring to the parent, and deliberately constrained to protect trust over time.

## What we believe

> Growth is a consequence of relationship, not the goal of play.

We're filling the gap between high-dopamine apps and passive content — calm-tech that makes daily care feel meaningful, not productive. No streaks. No checklists. No open internet. Just a pet to care for, every day.

## How we ship

AI-native team. Agents are first-class collaborators across product, marketing, design, and operations. PR-based workflows, markdown-first docs, skills as company assets. We move fast because we built the company for this from day one.
